## Reference

Edmunds, S. (2024, September 25). *KiwiSavers' $200,000 mistake*. RNZ. https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/business/528936/kiwisavers-200-000-mistake

## Summary

- RNZ reports many members switched from growth to conservative KiwiSaver funds during the March 2020 crash and did not switch back.
- Westpac data: 18,140 switch requests in that period; 27% never switched back.
- Long-run return gap cited (10-year averages): conservative 4.1%/yr vs growth 8.2%/yr vs aggressive 9.1%/yr.
- Illustrative projection from article: $25,000 starting balance in March 2020 -> about $615,423 (stay growth) vs $387,938 (stay conservative) by 2054, a gap of more than $225,000.

## Why it matters

- Real-world example of panic switching -> locking in losses and missing recovery.
- Supports [[Volatility Shocks Trigger Behavioral Mistakes in Equity Investors]].
- Supports [[All-equity Portfolios Outperform Glidepaths at Long Horizons]] limitation: volatility can trigger forced or behavioral selling.

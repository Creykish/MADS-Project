## Reference

Smith, D. (2021, June 17). *Millennials hit hardest by Covid KiwiSaver fund switch*. Stuff. https://www.stuff.co.nz/business/money/300334789/millennials-hit-hardest-by-covid-kiwisaver-fund-switch

## Summary

- Stuff reports millennials (age 26-35) were the most likely group to panic-switch KiwiSaver funds during the Feb-Apr 2020 Covid volatility period.
- FMA-commissioned PwC data cited: this group made 5x more switches than normal, and 21x more switches into lower-risk funds.
- Core mechanism: switching during a crash locks in losses, and many then miss the market recovery.
- Commentary in article points to herd effects, social-media influence, and low access to personalised financial advice for younger members.

## Why it matters

- Reinforces behavioral switching risk during volatility shocks.

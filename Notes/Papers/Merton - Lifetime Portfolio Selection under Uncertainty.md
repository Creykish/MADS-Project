## Summary

Optimized [[Life-cycle Asset Allocation]] for the continuous time case to maximise consumption utility. Finds a Constant allocation to risk is optimal (does not vary with time).
Consumption is a proportion of  wealth such that 
$$ 
W(t+1) = W(t)(1-C(t))\bf{Z}

$$
Risk scores:
Constant Relative Risk Aversion (CRRA) This is termed isoelastic utility.
$$ U(C) = \frac{C^{1-\gamma}}{1-\gamma} $$
